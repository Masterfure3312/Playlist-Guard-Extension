# Privacy Policy for Playlist Guard

**Last updated: August 26, 2026**

Playlist Guard is a browser extension designed to help users monitor changes in YouTube playlists, including videos that disappear, return, or are newly added.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Playlist Guard processes and how that information is handled.

---

## Information Playlist Guard Processes

Playlist Guard may process the following information when you choose to protect a YouTube playlist:

- Playlist title
- Playlist URL and playlist identifier
- Playlist thumbnail
- Video titles
- Video identifiers
- Channel names
- Video positions within playlists
- Playlist change history
- Missing, restored, and newly added video status
- User-selected automatic check intervals
- Interface preferences such as language and theme

---

## Local Storage

Playlist Guard stores its data locally in your browser using browser extension storage.

This includes:

- Protected playlists
- Playlist snapshots
- Missing video records
- Change history
- Extension settings
- Backup-related data

Playlist Guard does not require a Playlist Guard account.

---

## Data Collection

Playlist Guard does not operate its own analytics, advertising, tracking, or telemetry service.

Playlist Guard does not intentionally collect or transmit your stored playlist data to a Playlist Guard-controlled server.

---

## Communication With YouTube

Playlist Guard communicates with YouTube when necessary to retrieve playlist information and check for changes.

These requests are made directly from the extension to YouTube.

Your use of YouTube remains subject to Google's and YouTube's own terms and privacy policies.

---

## Third-Party Services

Playlist Guard may provide links that allow users to search for missing videos using services such as:

- YouTube
- Google Search

Opening those links is optional and is handled by the third-party service selected by the user.

Playlist Guard does not control the privacy practices of those services.

---

## Permissions

Playlist Guard may request browser permissions required for its core functionality.

These may include:

### Storage

Used to save protected playlists, change history, preferences, and extension data locally.

### Tabs

Used when the user asks Playlist Guard to open a playlist or related page.

### Alarms

Used to perform scheduled playlist checks according to the interval selected by the user.

### Access to YouTube

Playlist Guard requires access to YouTube pages in order to retrieve playlist information and check whether playlist contents have changed.

These permissions are used only for Playlist Guard functionality.

---

## Data Sharing

Playlist Guard does not sell user data.

Playlist Guard does not share stored playlist data with advertisers.

Playlist Guard does not use stored playlist information for advertising or user profiling.

---

## Backups

Playlist Guard allows users to export local extension data as a backup file.

Backup files are created at the user's request.

The user is responsible for storing and protecting exported backup files.

Playlist Guard can also import compatible backup files selected by the user.

---

## Data Deletion

Users can delete individual protected playlists from Playlist Guard.

Users may also remove locally stored extension data by:

- deleting protected playlists within Playlist Guard;
- clearing the extension's storage through the browser;
- uninstalling the extension.

Uninstalling the extension may remove locally stored Playlist Guard data depending on browser behavior.

---

## Children's Privacy

Playlist Guard is not specifically designed to collect information from children.

The extension does not intentionally collect personal information through its own servers because Playlist Guard does not operate a user account or remote data collection service.

---

## Security

Playlist Guard is designed to minimize data collection by keeping extension data local whenever possible.

However, no software or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Users should keep their browser and extension installation up to date.

---

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Playlist Guard's functionality or data practices change.

Updates will be published in this repository and the "Last updated" date will be revised.
