Built for playlist memory

YouTube may remove or hide videos from playlists without clearly telling you what disappeared. Playlist Guard keeps track for you.

01

Detect missing videos

Playlist Guard compares playlist snapshots and tells you which videos are no longer present.

02

Track restorations

If a previously missing video returns, Playlist Guard records that change instead of forgetting it.

03

Local history

Playlist information and change history are stored locally in your browser.

04

Independent schedules

Every protected playlist can use its own automatic check interval.

05

Manual checks

Check a playlist whenever you want without changing its next scheduled automatic check.

06

Backups and themes

Export your local data, restore backups, and choose between multiple interface themes.

Privacy first

Playlist Guard does not require an account and does not operate its own analytics, advertising, or tracking service. Playlist data is stored locally in your browser.
